# NEXUS V18 — Dark cinematic title sequence
Entering 0002 now triggers a seven-second, pitch-black cinematic motion-graphics sequence instead of a loading UI. Kinetic NEXUS lettering assembles letter by letter; three dimensional glass shards fly into place; thin crosshair and energy lines expand; then typography rushes through the camera and an ENTER title resolves before the screen reveals the hub. Procedural synthesized sound cues play after the user initiates the animation. No progress bar, percentages, or fake loading messages. The sound toggle and reduced-motion support remain. All other NEXUS V17 files and features are preserved.
